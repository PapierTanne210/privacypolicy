# Privacy Policy — HyperLite

**Last updated:** August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data **HyperLite** ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. It applies to any Discord server that has added the Bot.

By using a server that has HyperLite installed, or by interacting with the Bot directly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

---

## 1. Who This Applies To

HyperLite is intended for use on Discord in accordance with [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines), which require all users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Discord User ID | Identifying members for leveling and other features |
| Discord Server (Guild) ID | Keeping each server's data and settings separate |
| Discord Role IDs | Assigning/removing level-reward roles |
| XP and level per user, per server | Powering the leveling system, `/rank`, and the leaderboard |
| Welcome message configuration set by server staff (channel, message text, on/off toggles) | Running the welcome system |

HyperLite does **not** read or store the text content of your messages, and does **not** access presence/online-status data. It only observes that a message was sent (to award XP) — the content itself is never inspected, logged, or stored.

We do not collect Discord passwords, email addresses, payment information, or IP addresses.

---

## 3. How We Use Data

We use the data above only to operate HyperLite's features: the welcome system, leveling/leaderboards, and related admin-panel configuration. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or to train machine learning/AI models.

---

## 4. Where Data Is Stored, and For How Long

All persistent data (XP/levels, welcome configuration) is stored in a local database on the server(s) we use to host the Bot. It is not shared with, or accessible to, any third party.

- Data is **not** deleted on a fixed 30-day schedule — it is kept for as long as the Bot remains in your server, since leveling data is meant to persist over time.
- When a member leaves a server, their leveling data tied to that server is automatically deleted.
- The storage disk/volume used to host this data is encrypted at rest.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your data (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR).

To request deletion of your data, contact us on Discord (**@papiertanne210**) or join our support server at **discord.gg/CHSyA9eZAK** and open a ticket. Upon a verified request, we will delete your stored guild ID, user ID, XP, and level records within **7 days**.

Removing the Bot from a server, or leaving a server that uses it, stops any further data collection about you by the Bot.

---

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures, including disk-level encryption, to protect stored data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

HyperLite is not directed at children under the age required by Discord's Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users below this age.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of a server with the Bot installed after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
